Where I've led IT

Every IT problem is one of three things

Most companies guess. Guessing is expensive.

When IT is slow, unstable or over budget, leadership usually blames whatever is most visible. The real cause is often somewhere else.

01

The system

The technology itself is failing. ERP crashes at peak, releases break things, and the business builds spreadsheets to work around it.

02

The team

The people are capable but buried. Too much firefighting, unclear ownership, and no room to fix the root cause.

03

The budget

The money goes to the wrong places, and IT can't make the case for the right ones in terms a CFO will fund.
