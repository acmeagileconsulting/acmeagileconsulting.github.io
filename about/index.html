About

Hi, I'm Dennis Wong.

I run the technology that runs the business.

I was the divisional CIO for a Best Buy business run as its own company, and head of IT operations for a $500M private equity backed brand, 5.11 Tactical.

I've owned the whole stack, from ERP, warehouse and order systems to infrastructure, security and the budget. I've rescued failing systems and stalled programs, built a platform that generated $350M, and defended every dollar in front of the CEO and CFO.

Today I help mid-market companies figure out whether their IT problem is the system, the team or the budget, and then fix it.

On the warehouse floor. This is where IT either works or it doesn't.
