5.11 Tactical · System

Stopped up to $1M a day in lost revenue

Problem

D365 ERP failed every time a promotion drove peak volume. The workaround was to shut off inventory flows and clean up by hand afterward.

What I did

Pulled the ERP admins off the ticket queue, moved vendors from feature work to performance work, and drove the root cause across IT, the business and vendors.

Result

Stable under peak load in 90 days. Promotions ran with every system on, and the business trusted IT again.

5.11 Tactical · Team

Quarterly ERP releases to weekly

Problem

Every D365 release took weeks of manual testing by business users, plus travel. Features waited months to ship.

What I did

Found the real bottleneck was manual QA, not deployment. Hired an automation engineer, built automated regression testing and a CI/CD pipeline in Azure DevOps.

Result

Weekly releases. 2,000+ hours a year of manual testing eliminated. $250K to $300K a year saved in labor and travel.

5.11 Tactical · Budget

$2.5M+ a year found in labor tracking

Problem

IT labor wasn't tracked well enough to claim CAPEX treatment or R&D tax credits. Money was left on the table every year.

What I did

Built a simple time tracking tool on Microsoft tools the company already owned, and worked with Finance to classify eligible work.

Result

$2.5M+ in annual CAPEX and tax benefit, with zero new software cost.

Best Buy · System

$350M in new revenue from a platform built in-house

Problem

The in-store and online trade-in platform couldn't scale with the business.

What I did

Rebuilt it in-house with my own team, at zero vendor cost.

Result

$350M in incremental revenue. Customers spent about 3.5x their trade-in credit.

Best Buy · Team

Unstuck a stalled ERP program in under 24 months

Problem

A multi-year ERP modernization across supply chain, warehouses and eCommerce had stalled, in a division moving $4B+ in goods a year.

What I did

Moved a 40 to 50 person organization to Agile and Azure DevOps, prioritized by business value, shipped in increments, and brought all L2 and L3 support in-house.

Result

Full value delivered in under 24 months. Four warehouses consolidated to two. Company-wide incident calls cut from weekly to quarterly.
